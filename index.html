The Simple Solution

PR4Shopping is a digital rewards portal designed specifically for Park & Ride passengers. We connect sustainable transit directly to local commerce through a verified, secure platform.

What it does

It turns a standard bus journey into a rewarding local shopping experience. Passengers unlock exclusive value simply by choosing to use the Park & Ride.

How it works

The app uses QR codes at Park & Ride sites, and GPS if required, to confirm a user is at the site. Passengers browse and open offers during their journey. At a partner business, they simply click the offer and show the discount code to staff to redeem it.

When it happens

Activation is instant. The app guides the passenger from the moment they arrive at the terminus right through to the point of sale in your local shops.
