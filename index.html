The Simple Solution

Connecting sustainable travel to local commerce.

What it does

PR4Shopping is a digital rewards portal designed specifically for Park & Ride passengers. It turns a standard bus journey into a rewarding local shopping experience.

How it works

The app uses secure GPS to verify the user is at a Park & Ride site. Passengers browse exclusive offers on their way into town and redeem them directly at the counter.

When it happens

Verification is instant. The app guides the passenger from the moment they arrive at the terminus right through to the point of sale in your local shops.

Why join the scheme?

Helping Councils and Businesses grow together.

1
For Councils: Improve city air quality by making Park & Ride the preferred choice. We provide the technology to support your transport and environmental targets.
2
For Businesses: Connect with thousands of shoppers every single morning. PR4Shopping puts your current offers directly onto the phones of incoming passengers.
3
Verified Security: Our unique digital handshake ensures tokens are only valid for users physically verified at your transit sites, protecting the integrity of the scheme.
4
National Rollout: We are currently selecting 12 Lead UK Cities to pioneer this direct approach to town centre regeneration for 2026.
