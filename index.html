The Simple Solution

How PR4Shopping connects transit to trade.

What it does

PR4Shopping is a digital rewards portal that only unlocks when a passenger is at a Park & Ride site. It turns "waiting for the bus" into an opportunity to discover exclusive local discounts.

How it works

Using secure GPS verification, the app confirms the user is a genuine P&R passenger. They browse offers on the journey and show a unique "Verified" token to staff at the shop counter.

When it happens

Activation is instant. From the moment the passenger arrives at the terminus to the moment they step into the shop, the app guides them toward participating local merchants.

Why join the scheme?

Clear benefits for Councils and Businesses.

1
For Councils: Improve city air quality by encouraging Park & Ride use. We provide a zero-capital digital bridge that supports your environmental and transport targets.
2
For Businesses: Stop losing customers to out-of-town malls. Get your brand in front of thousands of high-intent shoppers the moment they start their journey.
3
Verified Footfall: Unlike generic discount sites, our tokens are only valid for users physically verified at your transit sites, ensuring the scheme supports local infrastructure.
4
National Momentum: Join the 2026 rollout. We are currently selecting 12 Lead UK Cities to pioneer this direct approach to town centre regeneration.
